BuildGreen

SUSTAINABLE ENGINEERING FRAMEWORK

The Built Environment Accounts for 37%–40% of Global Carbon Emissions.

BuildGreen is a multi-vector lifecycle assessment framework engineered to quantify initial structural carbon loading. By parsing empirical data across global inventory ledgers, this tool models complex cementitious binder substitutions, transportation logistics, and reinforcing steel matrices. It empowers structural engineers and procurement officers to make carbon-optimal decisions during the early blueprint phase, aligning regional infrastructure with international net-zero benchmarks.

Methodological System Architecture

The calculations engineered into BuildGreen parse empirical carbon data coefficients compiled across the global Inventory of Carbon and Energy (ICE) Ledger and standard structural directives published by the American Concrete Institute (ACI). Transport freight variables model a standard heavy-vehicle logistics coefficient calibrated at 0.12 kg of CO₂ per metric ton-kilometer. Ecosystem sequestration data sets adopt the US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) parameters modeling localized organic absorption rates.
